BUSINESS MONEY BASICS

Six ideas every owner should know

Plain-language explanations of what banks and advisers keep saying.

Revenue vs profit

Revenue is all the money you receive. Profit is what is left after you pay every cost. A business with ₹50,000 in sales and ₹48,000 in costs earns only ₹2,000.

Credit score

A number (usually 300 to 900) that shows lenders how reliably you repay. Pay on time, avoid maxing out cards and check your report for mistakes.

Collateral

An asset pledged as security for a loan. Many women own little property, so look for collateral-free loans and credit guarantee schemes.

Working capital vs term loan

Working capital covers daily needs like stock and wages. A term loan buys longer-term items like machines. Match the loan to the need.

Informal lenders

Money lenders can charge very high interest and keep your papers or assets. Compare with a bank, SHG or scheme loan before you accept.

Business insurance

Insurance can protect stock, equipment and your income from fire, theft or illness. Buy only what you understand, directly from a trusted insurer.

FACT CHECK

Myths vs facts

Common beliefs that can cost you money.

MYTH

An agent can get my loan approved faster.

FACT

Schemes are applied for directly at banks and official portals. Agents who demand money may be cheating you.

MYTH

Checking my own credit score lowers it.

FACT

Checking your own score is a soft enquiry and does not reduce it.

MYTH

A bank officer may call to ask for my OTP to fix a problem.

FACT

Banks never ask for your OTP, PIN or password. Anyone who does is a fraudster.

MYTH

A family account is enough, I do not need my own.

FACT

An account in your own name gives you control over your earnings and builds your own financial record.

MYTH

I need property to get a business loan.

FACT

Several schemes offer collateral-free loans for small businesses. Good records and a clear plan help a lot.

MYTH

Small businesses do not need records.

FACT

Records prove income, help you price correctly and are often required for loans.

Quick Glossary

EMI
A fixed monthly payment that repays a loan with interest.
Collateral
An asset you pledge to a lender as security for a loan.
Working capital
Money for day-to-day running costs such as stock, wages and rent.
Margin money
Your own contribution towards the cost of a project.
Udyam
The free government registration for micro, small and medium enterprises.
Subsidy
Financial help from the government that reduces what you pay or borrow.
Credit guarantee
Cover that protects the lender if a borrower cannot repay, so banks can lend without collateral.
Nominee
The person you name to receive your account or policy money if something happens to you.
