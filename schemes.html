LOAN READINESS CHECK

Are you ready to apply for a loan?

Banks look at the same few things. See where you stand and what to fix first.

1. Do you have a bank account in your own name?

2. Is your business registered on Udyam?

3. Do you have a written business plan or project report?

4. Do you keep records of your sales and expenses (last 3 months)?

5. Do you know exactly how much you need and what you will buy (with quotations)?

6. Do you pay bills and EMIs on time, and have you checked your credit score?

7. Are your Aadhaar, PAN and address proof ready, with your mobile linked?

8. Can you put some of your own money into the business?

HOW TO APPLY

Five steps from scheme to sanction

Studies show women often drop out because the process feels complex. Take it one step at a time.

1

Choose a scheme

Use the Scheme Finder and read the official portal for the latest rules.

2

Register and open accounts

Get your Udyam certificate and a bank account in your own name.

3

Prepare plan and papers

Make a one-page plan and tick off the document checklist below.

4

Apply directly

Apply at a bank branch or on the official portal. Do not pay anyone to guarantee approval.

5

Follow up

Note your application number, visit the branch every week or two, and ask politely for status.

If your application is rejected

Ask the bank for the reason in writing. Fix that one issue, such as missing records or a low credit score, and apply again or try another bank. If you believe you were treated unfairly, you can raise a complaint on the government grievance portal, pgportal.gov.in.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Get your papers ready

Missing documents are one of the most common reasons applications stall. Requirements vary by scheme and bank.

POPULAR SCHEMES FOR WOMEN

Schemes That Can Help You Start

Three widely used central government schemes for women who want to start or expand a business.

Stand-Up India

BEING REVAMPED

Bank loans to help women and SC/ST entrepreneurs start a new business. The original scheme ended on 31 March 2025 and a revamped version with higher loan limits has been announced.

  • For first-time (new) enterprises
  • Check the portal for the latest rules and limits
Visit Official Portal

PM Mudra Yojana

COLLATERAL-FREE

Collateral-free loans for small and micro enterprises, available in stages based on your business needs.

  • Loans up to ₹20 lakh across Shishu, Kishore, Tarun and Tarun Plus
  • For new and existing small businesses
Visit Official Portal

PMEGP

CREDIT-LINKED SUBSIDY

Credit-linked subsidy support for setting up new micro enterprises and creating self-employment opportunities.

  • Higher subsidy rate for women applicants
  • Applied online through the PMEGP portal
Visit Official Portal

Last reviewed: November 2026. Loan limits, subsidy rates and rules can change. Always confirm the latest details on the official portal or at your bank branch.

MORE SUPPORT FOR WOMEN

Other Schemes Worth Knowing

Programmes for rural women, savings for girls and women-focused enterprise support.

Lakhpati Didi

SELF-HELP GROUPS

Helps rural women in self-help groups build skills, credit access and market links to earn ₹1 lakh or more every year.

  • Run under DAY-NRLM (Ministry of Rural Development)
  • Join through your local self-help group
Visit Official Portal

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

GIRL CHILD SAVINGS

A government-backed savings account for a girl child, opened by a parent or guardian before she turns ten.

  • Interest rate is set by the government every quarter (8.2% at last check)
  • Deposits up to ₹1.5 lakh a year
Visit India Post

Mahila Udyam Nidhi & Udyogini

WOMEN-FOCUSED LOANS

Loan programmes designed for women starting or growing small enterprises, offered through banks and development institutions.

  • Terms differ from lender to lender
  • Ask your bank or SIDBI for current details
Visit SIDBI

TREAD

TRAINING & SUPPORT

Trade Related Entrepreneurship Assistance and Development: training and credit-linked support for women entrepreneurs.

  • Usually reached through NGOs and training institutions
  • Run by the Ministry of MSME
Visit MSME Ministry

The Mahila Samman Savings Certificate no longer accepts new deposits (closed after 31 March 2025). Existing accounts continue until maturity.
